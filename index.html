Scrubbers

Our first game. You are X, an antivirus program traveling from program to program through a living digital world, cleaning out infections one sector at a time.

  • Travel the net. Start in X's home network, then venture out onto the wider internet — and into the network of the company behind it all.
  • Collect fragments. Defeated viruses break down into chip fragments. Combine them into chips.
  • Build your grid. Slot chips into your grid to grow stronger, your way.

The studio

Tuna Cat Studios is an independent, design-first studio. We believe a game should be fair to its players: readable, honest about what it asks of you, and respectful of your time. We self-publish, and we answer to our players — not a publisher's calendar.
